# References

- **Li et al. (2020)** — *A Review of Tactile Information: Perception and Action Through Touch*  
  Review of tactile sensing in robotics, including contact, object, and action-level information.  
  [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/9136877)  
  DOI: [10.1109/TRO.2020.3003230](https://doi.org/10.1109/TRO.2020.3003230)

- **Yuan, Dong and Adelson (2017)** — *GelSight: High-Resolution Robot Tactile Sensors for Estimating Geometry and Force*  
  Optical tactile sensor that measures deformation to infer geometry, force, and slip.  
  [Paper](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/17/12/2762)  
  DOI: [10.3390/s17122762](https://doi.org/10.3390/s17122762)

- **James and Lepora (2021)** — *Slip Detection for Grasp Stabilization With a Multifingered Tactile Robot Hand*  
  Uses tactile slip detection to stabilise grasps and prevent dropped objects.  
  [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/9252140)  
  DOI: [10.1109/TRO.2020.3031245](https://doi.org/10.1109/TRO.2020.3031245)

- **Mao et al. (2024)** — *Multimodal tactile sensing fused with vision for dexterous robotic housekeeping*  
  Combines tactile and visual sensing for grasping, slip detection, and object recognition.  
  [Nature Communications](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-51261-5)  
  DOI: [10.1038/s41467-024-51261-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-51261-5)

- **Zenha et al. (2025)** — *Let’s DENSE: a novel protocol for efficiently collecting dense and diverse data for tactile slip detection in robotic grasping*  
  Data-collection protocol for diverse gripper-object interactions and tactile slip detection.  
  [npj Robotics](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44182-025-00055-y)  
  DOI: [10.1038/s44182-025-00055-y](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44182-025-00055-y)

- **Hansen et al. (2022)** — *Visuotactile-RL: Learning Multimodal Manipulation Policies with Deep Reinforcement Learning*  
  Reinforcement learning with visual and tactile feedback; compares multimodal and unimodal baselines.  
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICRA46639.2022.9812019)

- **Huang et al. (2025)** — *3D-ViTac: Learning Fine-Grained Manipulation with Visuo-Tactile Sensing*  
  Fuses visual and tactile data in a 3D representation for fine-grained bimanual manipulation.  
  [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.24091)  
  DOI: [10.48550/arXiv.2410.24091](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2410.24091)

- **Liu et al. (2025)** — *VTDexManip: A Dataset and Benchmark for Visual-tactile Pretraining and Dexterous Manipulation with Reinforcement Learning*  
  Dataset and benchmark for visual-tactile pretraining and dexterous manipulation.  
  [ICLR 2025](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/e19b6f65791e350347bcff8a3955cb5b-Abstract-Conference.html)

- **Su et al. (2024)** — *Sim2Real Manipulation on Unknown Objects with Tactile-based Reinforcement Learning*  
  Studies tactile reinforcement learning in simulation and transfer to real robots.  
  [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/10611113)  
  DOI: [10.1109/ICRA57147.2024.10611113](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICRA57147.2024.10611113)

- **Chen et al. (2022)** — *Visuo-Tactile Transformers for Manipulation*  
  Uses self- and cross-modal attention to combine visual and tactile feedback.  
  [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.00121)  
  DOI: [10.48550/arXiv.2210.00121](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2210.00121)

- **Chen et al. (2026)** — *Multi-Modal Manipulation via Multi-Modal Policy Consensus*  
  Adaptive multimodal policy approach designed to balance vision and tactile information.  
  [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.23468)  
  DOI: [10.48550/arXiv.2509.23468](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2509.23468)

- **Zhou et al. (2026)** — *TouchWorld: A Predictive and Reactive Tactile Foundation Model for Dexterous Manipulation*  
  Uses tactile feedback for prediction and rapid correction during contact-rich manipulation.  
  [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07287)  
  DOI: [10.48550/arXiv.2607.07287](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.07287)
